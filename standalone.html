02 / The social issue

When public trust
runs underwater.

The proposal responds to alleged irregularities in public infrastructure—and the consequences for the people it should protect.

01

Alleged irregularities

Reports of unfinished, substandard, or “ghost” flood control projects in vulnerable regions.

02

Resources at risk

Funds intended for community protection may be diverted from essential public works.

03

A human cost

Communities face displacement, property loss, and preventable safety risks when protection fails.

03 / Why this demands action

Beyond the budget.
Into everyday lives.

Accountability connects responsible public spending to safety, dignity, and a stronger civic culture.

01 / HUMAN DIGNITY

Lives are
on the line.

Failed infrastructure threatens people’s safety and dignity during climate disasters. The consequences extend far beyond accounting.

02 / VULNERABILITY

Protection must
reach everyone.

Marginalized communities bear severe consequences when flood defenses fail. Their needs belong at the center of public decisions.

03 / CITIZENSHIP

Critique with care.
Act with evidence.

Constructive civic advocacy encourages informed participation, respectful dialogue, and accountability.

04 / The moral foundation

Entrusted to us.
Meant for all.

“Pondo ng Bayan,
Para sa Bayan.”

Two Catholic Social Teaching principles guide the proposal’s response.

✳

The
Common Good

Social conditions should allow everyone to live with dignity. Reliable flood protection safeguards public welfare and community well-being.

↗

Responsible
Stewardship

Public funds are entrusted resources. Leaders and contractors have a responsibility to manage them for the benefit of all.

05 / Project objectives

Awareness is a start.
Participation is the goal.

Move from understanding an issue to practicing informed and responsible citizenship.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Raise
awareness.

Explain alleged flood control irregularities and their implications for communities using clear, accessible information.

02 / QUESTION

Promote
accountability.

Encourage responsible spending through verified information, credible sources, and transparency.

03 / PARTICIPATE

Activate
student voices.

Inspire students and youth to evaluate information critically and participate in civic life.

06 / Who the advocacy serves

Informed youth.
Stronger communities.

Primary beneficiaries

Students & youth

Social media users developing critical media literacy, an understanding of public budgets, and a sense of civic responsibility.

Secondary beneficiaries

Flood-prone communities

Communities that may ultimately benefit from stronger public oversight and sustained demand for effective public works.

A platform for truth.

02

Curate credible content

Publish infographics, summaries of verified news, and official reports.

03

Connect to CST

Share educational insights on stewardship and the common good.

04

Keep dialogue open

Moderate respectful, evidence-based discussions among followers.
